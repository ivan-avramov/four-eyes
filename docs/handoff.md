## Evaluation harness for the skill (owed, 2026-10-21)

The operator wants the skill's quality measured, not asserted: an evaluation harness that runs four-eyes on a fixed set of problems and scores the run. Candidate measures, each read from the run's artifacts: rounds dispatched against rounds the problem needed; growth of the specification across rounds against the goal paragraph (words, mechanisms, acceptance criteria), with excess over the goal as the reviewers list it; findings adopted with their mechanism against adopted for their problem; residuals named against residuals built against; operator interventions during the run; helper cost and wall time; whether the final change meets the goal paragraph as a cold reader judges it. A first case: the juno-ai run of 2026-10-21 recorded in `docs/runs/2026-10-21-closure-patch.md`, whose artifacts under the evidence repository's `sessions/2026-10-21-session-E/` hold every brief, member output and disposition ledger for three rounds, the operator's cut at Go, and the implementation and test deliveries; rerunning the same specification request under 0.6.2 against the same panel gives the before and after. The harness belongs beside the skill, not in an adopter's repository.
