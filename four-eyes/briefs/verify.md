# Verifier brief: what the review-panel brief must carry

The verifier is review-panel in `converge` mode; the brief follows review-panel's own rules (freeze the material, delimit it, carry no verdict of yours, end with its return contract verbatim). For a four-eyes verification it must also carry, in this order:

1. The operator's original request, in their words or a faithful paraphrase, so a requirement the specification dropped can be challenged.
2. Settled constraints, each marked settled by whom (operator, repository rules, decision record).
3. Your assumptions, marked as yours.
4. The specification as the delimited material, including its acceptance criteria and interfaces.
5. Your question: ask for disconfirming cases against the acceptance criteria, the settled constraints and the original request; ask where an agent following the specification literally would fail, where a role could be filled wrongly, and what the specification leaves ambiguous. Never ask for confirmation.
6. The review-panel return contract for converge mode, verbatim, as the last text.

Context: the least each member's runtime supports, named truthfully; `web` when the specification claims external behaviour, and only then may the question ask members to check those claims and cite sources with dates.
