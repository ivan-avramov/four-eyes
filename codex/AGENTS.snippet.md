## four-eyes

For any change touching security, authentication, permissions, secrets or a data boundary, and for any multi-file change, new repository or component, or architecture, follow the installed `four-eyes` skill (found in the repository's `.agents/skills/four-eyes` or in `~/.agents/skills/four-eyes`; it requires `review-panel`). Fill its roles as it says, record the evidence of the models that ran, and stop to ask the operator with a recommendation when a role cannot be filled as intended. If the skill is installed in neither place, say so and ask before proceeding.
