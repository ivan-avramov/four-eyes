## four-eyes

Not for a session dispatched as a helper (implementer, test-writer or panel member) in another run.

For non-trivial development work (multi-file changes, new repositories or components, architecture, substantial single-file changes) and always for anything touching security, authentication, permissions, secrets or a data boundary, the installed `four-eyes` skill applies: `SKILL.md` and `briefs.md` from the repository's `.agents/skills/four-eyes` or from `~/.agents/skills/four-eyes` (it requires `review-panel`). Strict mode for security and architecture, light otherwise. Small unambiguous edits outside those areas are exempt. If the skill is installed in neither place, say so and ask before proceeding.
